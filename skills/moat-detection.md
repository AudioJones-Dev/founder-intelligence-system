# Moat Detection

## Purpose
Detect emerging or eroding structural advantages in product, distribution, data, workflow, or organizational learning.

## Core Principle
Moats are dynamic. Advantage exists only if it compounds faster than competitors can replicate.

## Inputs
- Customer behavior signals
- Product usage and retention patterns
- Competitive observations
- Internal capability change logs

## Outputs
- Current moat hypothesis map
- Strength and decay indicators
- Priority interventions

## Detection Logic
Compare claimed advantage against evidence of compounding, defensibility, and transferability. Flag weak claims with low evidence density.

## Operational Questions
- Which advantage is actually compounding?
- Where is imitation risk highest?
- What capability investment increases defensibility fastest?

## AI Agent Usage
Use in quarterly strategy reviews and before major capital or hiring allocations.

## Failure Modes
- Confusing temporary performance spikes for durable advantage
- Treating brand narrative as structural defensibility
- Ignoring operational fragility under scale

## Related Skills
- ghost-note-detection
- decision-compression

## Compression Summary
Differentiate narrative advantage from compounding defensibility.
