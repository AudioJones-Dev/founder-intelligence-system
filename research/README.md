# Research

Supporting research notes, market scans, source analysis, and evidence files. The substrate that informs doctrine and framework authorship.

## What Belongs Here

- Market scans referenced by doctrine or frameworks
- Comparative studies between frameworks (ours and external)
- Citations and source analyses for doctrine assumptions
- Notes on adjacent literature (negotiation, neuroplasticity, decision theory, etc.)

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Raw transcripts (those go in `transcripts/`)
- Structured extractions (those go in `extractions/`)
- Filled briefs (those go in `examples/`)
- Working drafts of new skills or frameworks (those go in the layer folder with `status: draft`)

## Naming Convention

`[YYYY-MM-DD]-[topic-slug].md` or `[topic-slug].md` for evergreen notes

## Status

This layer is intentionally empty in v0.1. Research notes will be added as doctrine and framework authorship requires supporting evidence.
