# Transcripts

Raw source material — interviews, podcasts, founder calls, sales calls, strategy sessions.

## Authoring Rules

- Plain text or markdown, untouched
- No interpretation, summary, or extraction (that work belongs in `extractions/`)
- Filename: `[YYYY-MM-DD]-[source-slug].md`
- The first lines of each transcript should include: source type, participants, date, duration, and any access notes

## Schema

Transcripts are not schema-validated — they are raw substrate. Their derived extractions are validated.

## Status

This layer is intentionally empty in v0.1. Source material will be added as it is acquired and authorized for inclusion in the canonical substrate.
