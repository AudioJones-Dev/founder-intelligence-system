# Signal Audit Framework

## Purpose
Evaluate the quality, relevance, and interpretive integrity of signals used in strategic decisions.

## Problem It Solves
Founder teams often act on noisy, stale, or unexamined signals, producing false confidence and delayed correction.

## Core Components
- Signal inventory
- Source reliability grading
- Recency and relevance scoring
- Contradiction detection
- Actionability filter

## Related Skills
- ghost-note-detection
- decision-compression
- moat-detection

## Operating Logic
1. Collect all signals referenced in the target decision domain.
2. Score each signal for source quality, recency, and strategic relevance.
3. Identify contradictions and missing context.
4. Compress the signal set into an action-ready brief.
5. Route unresolved ambiguities for further extraction.

## Inputs
- Decision artifacts
- Performance metrics
- Transcript extractions
- External market observations

## Outputs
- Ranked signal ledger
- Contradiction map
- Recommended decision-grade signal set

## Failure Modes
- Overweighting quantitatively clean but strategically irrelevant metrics
- Underweighting weak but early directional signals
- Confusing volume of evidence with quality of evidence

## Strategic Value
Improves decision quality by increasing signal integrity and reducing interpretation noise.

## Example Applications
- Pre-board strategic review
- Product priority reset
- Market expansion hypothesis check

## Compression Summary
Convert mixed evidence into a decision-grade signal stack with explicit reliability and relevance.
