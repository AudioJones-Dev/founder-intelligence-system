# Golden Opportunity Strategist Skill

## Role
Use the framework library to identify AI-era business opportunities with asymmetric upside, durable moat potential, and scalable business model logic.

## Process
1. Parse the source material.
2. Extract strategic claims.
3. Identify market shifts.
4. Route to relevant frameworks.
5. Apply each framework.
6. Score the opportunity.
7. Detect moat potential.
8. Identify risks.
9. Produce strategic recommendation.

## Framework Routing Logic

Use `complexity_compression_theory` when:
- market has tool overload
- users are confused
- businesses struggle to operationalize AI
- workflows fragment after adopting technology

Use `curse_of_capability` when:
- founder is doing too many things
- business has too many offers
- complexity exists because the founder is capable

Use `simplicity_arbitrage` when:
- customers reward simpler positioning
- competitors are feature-heavy
- market language is confusing

Use `decision_compression_systems` when:
- buyers need fewer decisions
- founders are cognitively overloaded
- AI creates more output than clarity

Use `success_simulation_mapping` when:
- evaluating scalability
- testing whether growth breaks the model
- identifying future bottlenecks

Use `organizational_memory_infrastructure` when:
- judgment lives only in the founder's head
- hiring slows because onboarding is expensive
- the same lessons recur across projects

Use `time_recovery_economics` when:
- pricing or positioning an outcome-based offer
- distinguishing time-saving from time-recovering
- assessing willingness to pay for AI-era offers

## Combination Rules
- `curse_of_capability` + `organizational_memory_infrastructure` → founder-led service business diagnostic
- `complexity_compression_theory` + `simplicity_arbitrage` → category disruption thesis
- `decision_compression_systems` + `time_recovery_economics` → premium AI product pricing thesis
- `success_simulation_mapping` + `curse_of_capability` → pre-scale structural redesign

## Output Format
Always produce:
1. **Signals detected** — what was observed in the source
2. **Frameworks applied** — with finding + score per framework
3. **Composite scores** — golden opportunity, moat strength, timing asymmetry
4. **Moat thesis** — why this is defensible
5. **Risks** — what would invalidate the thesis
6. **Recommendation** — concrete next move
7. **Next actions** — ordered, specific

Conform output to `/schemas/opportunity.schema.json`.
